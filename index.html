Biography

A little about me

I am James Sunday, a 100 Level Cyber Security student at MIVA Open University. This semester I began learning web development, and I have enjoyed seeing a page I wrote in a text editor come alive in the browser.

I like learning by building. Each small project teaches me something new, from laying out a page to making a button actually do something.
