Hello, I'm Adewole

I am a 100 Level Cyber Security student at MIVA Open University. Studying online has taught me to manage my own time, set my own deadlines and keep going even when a problem looks difficult.

Before this semester I had never written code. Now I can build a multi-page website with a working planner, a validated contact form and a few small projects of my own. I enjoy the moment when something finally works after hours of fixing mistakes.

Outside of class, I like following cyber security news and practising what I learn in safe lab environments. I believe consistent practice matters more than natural talent.

Career aspirations

Where I want to go

My goals

I want to help keep people and organisations safe online. This is the path I plan to follow:

  1. Build a strong foundation in cyber security throughout my degree.
  2. Begin as a security analyst, monitoring systems and responding to threats.
  3. Earn recognised entry-level certifications, starting with CompTIA Security+.
  4. Specialise as a penetration tester or security engineer.

Why this path?

Almost everything we do now happens online, which makes protecting data one of the most valuable jobs in technology. I am drawn to the challenge of thinking like an attacker so that I can build stronger defences.

Over time, I hope to lead a security team and teach others, especially students who are starting out the way I am now.
