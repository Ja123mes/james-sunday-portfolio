    Study tips

    Make the planner work for you

    1

    Be specific

    "Read chapter 3" is better than "study". A clear task is easier to start.

    2

    Use priorities

    Mark urgent deadlines as high priority so you see them first.

    3

    Review weekly

    Clear completed tasks every Friday and plan the week ahead.

    How the colours work

    • A red edge means high priority.
    • A purple edge means medium priority.
    • A green edge means low priority.
